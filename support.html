FAQ

Where is my data stored?
On your device only. We don't have servers that store your clients or appointments. See the Privacy Policy.

How do I move my clients to a new phone?
Go to Settings → Data & Backup → Export Backup and save the file. On the new phone, open the same screen and tap Restore from Backup.

How do I cancel a subscription?
Open iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Client Book.
