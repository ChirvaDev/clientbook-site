**A good birthday message for a client is short, personal and sent on the day — by text, from you, with their name in it.** Below are 50 messages you can copy and paste, grouped by tone and by service, plus a few birthday offer ideas that bring clients back without feeling pushy.

Replace **[Name]** with your client's first name, and **[Your name]** with yours.

## When and how to send birthday messages

| | What works best |
|---|---|
| **When** | On the birthday itself, in the morning (9–11 am) |
| **How** | A text message. It feels personal and almost everyone reads it. |
| **Length** | 1–3 sentences. It's a greeting, not a newsletter. |
| **From** | You, by name — not "the team" |
| **Offer** | Optional. If you include one, keep it simple and give it a deadline. |

The hard part isn't writing the message — it's remembering. Write each client's birthday on their [client record card]({{ '/client-record-card-template' | relative_url }}) or in your client app, and check it every morning.

## Birthday offer ideas

| Offer | Why it works |
|---|---|
| Free add-on (deep conditioning, nail art, brow tint, scalp massage) | Costs you little, feels generous |
| 10–20% off one visit during their birthday month | Simple and easy to understand |
| Free upgrade (gel instead of regular polish, longer massage) | Lets them try something they might book again |
| "Bring a friend" offer | Brings in a new client at the same time |
| A small gift at their next visit | Personal, and it doesn't train clients to wait for discounts |

Whatever you choose, give it a deadline ("valid this month") so it gets used, and offer the same thing to every client so nobody feels left out.

## Common mistakes

- **Sending it late.** A message three days after the birthday shows you forgot. If you missed it, say so: "Belated happy birthday, [Name]!" is still better than nothing.
- **Making it all about the offer.** Lead with the wishes. The offer is a bonus.
- **Group messages that look like group messages.** Always use their first name.
- **Texting at night or too early.** Mid-morning is safe in any time zone your clients are in.
- **Messaging clients who didn't share their birthday.** Only use birthdays clients gave you, and respect anyone who asked not to receive messages.

## Questions

**What do you write in a birthday message to a client?**
Their first name, a short wish and your name. If you know something personal — a trip, a new job — mention it. Add an offer only if it fits your business.

**Should I send a birthday text or email to clients?**
A text. It feels more personal, it's read within minutes, and it's how most beauty clients already talk to their pro. Email works better for big salons sending birthday offers to hundreds of clients.

**Is it OK to offer a birthday discount?**
Yes — many salons do. Keep it to one visit within the birthday month, so it brings clients in without lowering your prices for good.

**How do I remember all my clients' birthdays?**
Ask for the birthday on your client card at the first visit, then keep the list somewhere you check every day — a calendar, a client app with birthday reminders, or a printed list for each month.
